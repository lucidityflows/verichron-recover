# UI-Collectors Product Strategy: The "Flow State" Workstation

**Date:** January 4, 2026
**Version:** 1.0
**Target Persona:** Debt Collectors (Agents) & Team Leads
**Philosophy:** Flow State, Protected Autonomy, and Micro-Wins.

---

## 1. Executive Vision

While `ui-organizations` is the "Command Center" for strategy and oversight, `ui-collectors` is the **"Cockpit"** for execution.

**The Problem:** Traditional collection software is an obstacle course of spreadsheets, pop-ups, and compliance anxiety. Collectors spend 40% of their time clicking and 60% worrying about breaking rules.

**The Solution:** A **"Flow State" Workstation** that removes cognitive load. The system handles the "Who to call," "When to call," and "Is this legal?" decisions, allowing the collector to focus 100% on **negotiation and persuasion**.

**Core Design Principle:** "The System is the Safety Net." The UI physically prevents the user from making a compliance error, allowing them to work with speed and confidence.

---

## 2. The Four Operational Domains

### I. THE WORKBENCH (Execution)
The primary interface where 90% of the day is spent.
*   **Focus Mode:** A single-debt view that hides the queue and shows only the active account. Includes a "Next Best Action" prompt.
*   **The Smart Script:** Dynamic call scripting that updates based on the debt's age, balance, and state laws (fed by `ComplianceHub`).
*   **Rapid Disposition:** One-click outcome logging (e.g., "No Answer", "Left Voicemail", "PTP") that auto-advances the queue.

### II. THE SCOREBOARD (Motivation)
Combating burnout with visibility and gamification.
*   **Commission Ticker:** A live estimate of "Earnings Today" based on the `Financial Tiers` defined in `ui-organizations`.
*   **Daily Pace:** A simple progress bar: "You are $400 away from your daily goal."
*   **Streak Counter:** "7 PTPs in a row!" or "Top Performer this hour."

### III. THE PORTFOLIO (Ownership)
Managing the assigned book of business.
*   **My Queue:** A prioritized playlist of accounts generated by the `ResourceOptimizer` algorithms.
*   **Promise Tracker:** A dedicated view for monitoring upcoming and broken "Promises to Pay" (PTPs).
*   **Callback Calendar:** Personal schedule for agreed-upon follow-up calls.

### IV. THE NEGOTIATOR (Tools)
Assisting the deal-making process in real-time.
*   **Settlement Calculator:** Inputs an offer amount and instantly flashes **Green/Amber/Red** based on the `SettlementMatrix` rules.
*   **Payment Plan Builder:** Calculators for down payments and installment terms that validate against `PaymentPlanOrchestrator` limits.

## 3. Key Feature: The "Compliance Shield" (Runtime Enforcement)

Instead of *logging* violations, `ui-collectors` **prevents** them. This is the "Consumer-Grade" experience of compliance.

| Risk Scenario | Traditional Software | Verichron "Compliance Shield" |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Time Zones** | Collector checks clock, calculates time diff, maybe makes a mistake. | The "Call" button is **Disabled** and greyed out. Tooltip: *"Restricted: 9:04 PM Local Time"* |
| **Frequency** | Collector checks notes: "Did we call yesterday?" | "Call" button Disabled. Tooltip: *"7-in-7 Limit Reached. Next call allowed: Tuesday"* |
| **Do Not Call** | Collector misses the "DNC" flag note. | Primary phone number is masked/hidden. Action buttons removed. |
| **Settlement** | Collector offers 50% discount (unauthorized). | Input field turns Red. "Submit" button becomes "Request Approval". |

## 4. Key Feature: The "Negotiation Assistant" Sidebar

A persistent right-hand sidebar that acts as the collector's "Copilot" during a call.

**State 1: Pre-Call (The Briefing)**
*   "Account Age: 120 Days"
*   "Last Contact: 2 weeks ago (Hung Up)"
*   "Suggested Opener: 'I see you made a payment last year...'"

**State 2: In-Call (The Calculator)**
*   **Input:** Collector types "500" (Debtor's offer).
*   **Logic:** Checks `SettlementMatrix` (Balance vs. Age).
*   **Output:**
    *   *If Allowed:* Green Checkmark. "Authorized. Commission: $15.00".
    *   *If Borderline:* Amber Warning. "Requires Manager Approval. Click to escalate."
    *   *If Rejected:* Red X. "Below hard floor ($750). Counter with $800."

## 5. Technical Integration Points

*   **API Wiring:**
    *   `api-collectors`: Consumes `WorkQueue`, `Goals`, and `Performance` endpoints.
    *   `api-debts`: Reads `Debt` details, writes `Notes` and `Payment` transactions.
    *   `api-organizations`: Reads `Compliance` and `Strategy` configurations (Read-Only).
*   **Real-Time Events:**
    *   Listens for `DebtAssigned` (New item in queue).
    *   Listens for `ApprovalGranted` (Escalation resolved by Manager).

---

## 6. UX Aesthetic: "Focused Intensity"

*   **Visuals:** High contrast, large typography for critical numbers (Balance, Offer).
*   **Color Coding:**
    *   **Green:** Go / Safe / Money.
    *   **Amber:** Caution / Needs Approval.
    *   **Red:** Stop / Compliance Block.
*   **Interaction:** Keyboard shortcuts for everything (Alt+C = Call, Alt+D = Disposition).

---

## 7. Migration from V1 (Legacy Reference)

We will selectively harvest logic from `apps/ui-organizations-v1`, specifically:
*   **`ActionLogModal`**: Refine into the "Rapid Disposition" panel.
*   **`PaymentPlanCalculator`**: Integrate into the "Negotiation Assistant."
*   **`WorkQueueList`**: Rebuild with virtualization for the "My Queue" playlist.
*   **`GoalCard`**: Adapt into the "Scoreboard" widgets.

We will **discard**:
*   The dashboard-heavy "Admin-lite" views. Collectors need tools, not charts.
*   Manual filtering logic (The system should prioritize for them).
